# Evolution Lab: ideas (Build / Skip)

| # | Idea | Call | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Watering-hole forecast at the food-card decision: "Next ~X, range lo–hi", plus how much food the table needs | **Build** (done) | It's the main decision each round. The exact odds come from the unseen cards. |
| 2 | Threat marks: a red count on each species showing how many Carnivores can eat it, and a gold count showing how many species it can eat | **Build** (done) | It's the hardest part to see at the table. |
| 3 | "Who can eat whom" table (laptop side panel, and in the Log on phone) | **Build** (done) | Same reason, as a list with the reason each species is safe. |
| 4 | Tapping a species shows who can eat it and why the others can't | **Build** (done) | Teaches the defence traits by example. |
| 5 | Hint button: what the Hard computer would do | **Build** (done) | Study mode with no penalty. Can be turned off in Settings. |
| 6 | Study tab: your choices compared with the Hard computer's, the points lost per choice, a score race and a per-round table | **Build** (done) | Required: study mode with points lost. |
| 7 | Lab: food per round and survival for each trait, from Hard-vs-Hard games | **Build** (done) | Required: trait theory from simulation. |
| 8 | Lab: trait-pair heatmap (synergy "lift") | **Build** (done) | Required: synergy data. |
| 9 | Lab: "Can it eat?" calculator, where you set two species from your real table | **Build** (done) | Companion for a real game. |
| 10 | Lab: food deck chart, the expected food per card and per player count | **Build** (done) | Required: expected watering-hole food. |
| 11 | Quick Play variant switch (always on with 6 players) | **Build** (done) | It's an official variant. |
| 12 | Online seats: friends by room code, a mix of computers and people | **Build** (done) | Required by phase 2. |
| 13 | Computer speed setting (slow / normal / fast feeding turns) | **Build** (done) | Lets you follow attacks. |
| 14 | Bite flash on an attacked species, pop-in for new species | **Build** (done) | Shows what changed without reading the log. |
| 15 | Flight / Climate expansions | Skip | No public card list we could cross-check (see rules-check.md). |
| 16 | 1st-printing deck as a variant | Skip | Only three traits' old values are published. |
| 17 | Undo a sent move | Skip | It doesn't exist at the real table. It would also break online fairness. |
| 18 | Chat in online rooms | Skip | Not in the shared online layer. Friends talk anyway. |
| 19 | Real-table scorer (type in food bags and species) | Skip | The score is a simple sum, so a counter adds little. The "Can it eat?" calculator covers the hard part. |
| 20 | Free-time Intelligence before the first player feeds | Skip | A rare edge case. Documented as a simplification. |
